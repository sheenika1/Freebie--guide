What's inside

5 signs that tell you
it is time

Sign 01

You Have Started Hiding Who You Are

When love requires you to shrink, it is not love. If you have been editing your personality, your voice, your dreams — to keep the peace — this sign is for you.

Sign 02

You Feel More Alone Inside the Relationship Than You Would Outside of It

There is a particular kind of loneliness that only happens inside a relationship. Lying next to someone and feeling completely unseen. This sign names it.

Sign 03

You Have Started Planning a Life Without Them — and It Feels Like Relief

When imagining your future without someone brings peace instead of panic — pay attention. That feeling is telling you something important.

Sign 04

You Are Staying Out of Fear — Not Love

Fear of being alone. Fear of finances. Fear of starting over. If you removed all those fears — would you still choose to stay? This sign asks you that question.

Sign 05

You Have Lost Yourself — and You Know It

When you look in the mirror and barely recognize the woman looking back. When the things that used to light you up have gone quiet. She is still in there — this sign will remind you.
